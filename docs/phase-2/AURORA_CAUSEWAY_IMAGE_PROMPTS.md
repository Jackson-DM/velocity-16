## 01 — Environment Key Art

**Use case:** stylized-concept  
**Asset type:** game environment concept art  
**Primary request:** Aurora Causeway, an original futuristic hover-racing circuit suspended high above a storm-lit coastal megacity, functioning as luminous atmospheric energy infrastructure  
**Scene/backdrop:** black ocean outside the circuit, dense terraced city inside the bay, distant maglev traffic, maintenance drones, collector pylons, far storm lightning, and a broad cyan/sea-green/violet aurora ribbon  
**Subject:** a wide elevated racing causeway with midnight-indigo pavement, cold cyan outer energy rail, hot amber inner pressure rail, sparse sector pylons, and several small original hover racers for scale  
**Style/medium:** cinematic 2026 game environment concept art with HD retro-futurist clarity, crisp graphic silhouettes, sophisticated atmospheric depth, no resemblance to any existing racing franchise  
**Composition/framing:** wide 16:9 establishing view from slightly above and beside the track; clearly show a driveable broad course and layered city/ocean staging  
**Lighting/mood:** electric twilight, vibrant and alive, high energy, deep indigo shadows, luminous infrastructure, controlled storm drama  
**Color palette:** midnight indigo, electric cyan, hot amber, warm ivory, restrained violet, sea-green aurora, vermilion only for hazards  
**Constraints:** the road and collision edges must remain instantly legible; broad multiplayer-safe width; scenery stays outside the road silhouette; original designs only; no logos, advertisements, franchise references, watermark, or readable text

## 02 — Top-Down Surface And Lane Language

**Use case:** stylized-concept  
**Asset type:** top-down game track surface design sheet  
**Primary request:** an original top-down design study for Aurora Causeway showing a broad oval futuristic hover-racing track and its complete visual gameplay language  
**Scene/backdrop:** dark neutral presentation field with a small amount of surrounding elevated infrastructure and no decorative clutter  
**Subject:** midnight-indigo three-lane-width race surface; cyan outer rail; amber inner rail; warm-ivory directional lane dashes; cyan/gold forward-chevron boost pads; vermilion fractured-lattice electrical hazards; lime-white cellular recharge strip; bold start/finish bars; sparse sector pylons  
**Style/medium:** polished top-down game-environment concept sheet, crisp graphic shapes, material-aware painted surface, suitable for translating into a procedural Mode 7 texture  
**Composition/framing:** near-orthographic overhead view of the entire clockwise oval plus three enlarged surface-detail callouts without text  
**Lighting/mood:** even presentation lighting with emissive accents; vibrant but highly readable  
**Color palette:** midnight indigo road, cyan outside, amber inside, ivory navigation, cyan/gold boost, vermilion hazard, lime-white recharge  
**Constraints:** visibly support inside, center, and outside racing lines; hazards must leave multiple viable routes; recharge remains off the fastest line; no single-file chokepoints; no logos, words, labels, watermark, or resemblance to an existing game track

## 03 — Gameplay Framing Study

**Use case:** stylized-concept  
**Asset type:** in-game racing-view concept mockup  
**Primary request:** Aurora Causeway as seen during high-speed hover racing, proving that a vibrant living environment can remain readable in a low-resolution HD Mode 7 composition  
**Scene/backdrop:** elevated circuit over a coastal megacity at electric twilight; distant ocean storms, maglev lights, maintenance craft, city windows, energy pylons, and an aurora ribbon contained mainly above the horizon  
**Subject:** broad midnight-indigo road with unmistakable cyan outer rail, amber inner rail, ivory directional marks, and a clearly previewed staggered hazard choice; three original hover racers occupying separate viable lanes  
**Style/medium:** polished HD pixel-art-inspired game concept, chunky pixel precision with modern lighting, designed to read when reduced to 320x224, original visual identity  
**Composition/framing:** rear chase view close to the current Velocity-16 camera; road occupies the lower two-thirds; horizon and living skyline occupy the upper third; racers do not obscure every safe route  
**Lighting/mood:** vibrant, fast, exhilarating, alive, strong separation between foreground gameplay and softer background spectacle  
**Color palette:** restrained road values with high-clarity gameplay colors; richer but lower-contrast cyan, violet, and sea-green atmosphere  
**Constraints:** prioritize steering readability; show at least three practical lanes; no scenery crossing the road; no excessive particles, bloom, speed lines, UI, logos, readable text, franchise references, or watermark
